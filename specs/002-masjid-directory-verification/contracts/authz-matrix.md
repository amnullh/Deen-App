## How to read the table

| Cell | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `A` | allowed (on a `self` route: own account only; in the `public` column: anyone, signed in or not) |
| `S` | allowed with a fresh step-up (password plus fresh TOTP within 5 minutes for Operators, 10 minutes for admins and Muazzins) |
| `O` | allowed for a member of that Masjid only (own Masjid); `OS` adds step-up |
| `C` | allowed for the Operator assigned to that open case only (arbitrator or initiator where the machine says so); `CS` adds step-up |
| blank | denied (default deny) |
| `*` | the cell has a condition, listed under the table |

Scope column: `global` any object, `ownMasjid` membership required, `assignedCase` assignment required, `self` own account or own claim. A per-role scope is shown in brackets. The second-factor requirement is the `MFA` column: `yes` means a verified second factor on the session; `no` only for the pre-auth and public routes. A resource that does not exist and a resource the caller may not see both answer `404 not_found` with the same body.

## Never list (hand-written, independent of the table)

Each line must hold whatever the policy table says. The test suite asserts them separately (r7 section 1c), so a wrong policy row cannot hide.

1. **Other Masjids.** An admin or Muazzin of another Masjid, or of none, never reads or writes a Masjid's private data: profile, contacts, photos, versions, schedule rules through the admin routes, members, invitations, ownership transfers and the Masjid audit log. Every such call returns `404 not_found`, the same status, code and body shape as a missing id.
2. **Muazzin.** A Muazzin never writes the listing (names, location, profile, photos, schedule), never invites, removes or transfers, and never reads contacts, photos, versions or the audit log. A Muazzin sees the profile and the schedule of a Masjid they belong to, read-only.
3. **Reviewer.** A reviewer never suspends or reinstates a Masjid or an account, and never initiates or logs the call of a recovery (break-glass or operator recovery). A reviewer may only raise `flagForSuspension`. Reviewers do approve or deny second-factor resets and give the second approval of a break-glass, as state-machines.md 4b and 4c say (see Issues 1 and 2).
4. **Moderator.** A moderator never opens the claim queue, views evidence or committee contacts, decides a claim, an appeal or an arbitration, revokes a Masjid, or approves any recovery.
5. **Evidence and contacts.** Only the Operator assigned to an open case reads its evidence images and committee contact values: the reviewer assigned to the claim, the arbitrator for a Conflicted claim, the initiator for a break-glass case. A closed case answers 403 even to them. No list route, no admin route and no export ever returns contact values or image bytes. Admins and Muazzins never read either.
6. **Arbitration.** While a conflict is open no challenger has edit rights (they hold no membership) and a frozen incumbent cannot edit (`409 guard_failed`, `masjid_frozen`). An unfrozen Verified incumbent keeps editing (Issue 3).
7. **Super-admin.** A super-admin has no implicit evidence or contact read: the case must be assigned to them first, and the assignment is audited.
8. **No self-approval.** An Operator never approves their own break-glass (database CHECK `recovery_case_approver_not_initiator`), second-factor reset, ownership-transfer request or claim, and never decides an appeal against their own earlier decision (`not_original_decider`).
9. **Dead accounts.** A deleted, disabled or suspended account has no access: its sessions are closed at once (`session_epoch` + 1) and every route answers `401 unauthenticated`; sign-in answers `401 invalid_credentials` like any other failure.
10. **No shortcuts.** No route sets, shortens or skips the 48-hour break-glass delay, and no route exposes a system event (timeout, expiry, supersede, execute, remind, lock).
11. **Secrets.** No response contains a password hash, a TOTP secret after enrolment, a recovery code after the one-time display, a wrapped data key, an Operator name in an admin-facing audit row, or committee contact values outside `getCommitteeContacts` and `getRecoveryCaseContacts`.
12. **Public routes.** The two listener writes and the challenge never read or set a cookie, never carry coordinates or an identifier, and never reveal whether a Masjid, a duplicate or a prior report matched (same 202 body).
13. **Operator management.** Only a super-admin creates, re-roles or offboards Operators, publishes or rolls back an import, initiates a break-glass, enters recovery contacts or reads the audit chain status and coverage report.
14. **Anonymous callers.** A caller without a verified second factor reaches only the explicit allowlist of MFA-free operations listed above, nothing else.

## Completeness rule

1. Every route has exactly one policy row, keyed by `operationId`, with `x-roles`, `x-scope`, `x-mfa`, `x-step-up`, `x-audit-action`, `x-rate-limit` and `x-host`. The generator fails when any of them is missing, when a role or scope is not in the enumeration, when a public route is not MFA-free, when a state-changing operation has no audit action, when an `ownMasjid` route has no 404, or when a step-up route has no 403.
2. **Default deny.** A route with no policy row is not served: the router hook answers 403 and the completeness test fails the build. The test enumerates the real router, not only this file, so undocumented routes are caught (r7 1c). The same test crosses principals {anonymous, Muazzin of M1, admin of M1, admin of M2, suspended admin, reviewer, moderator, super-admin, deleted account} with targets {M1, M2, a missing id, evidence of M2}, expects the cell of this table plus the never list, and asserts that the M2 and the missing-id answers are byte-identical in status, code and body shape.
3. Policy rows are checked against this file: `x-host: api` appears only under `/v1/public/*`; every `/v1/public/*` route is MFA-free, cookie-free and has the `public_write` tier; every `x-events` entry is an API event of state-machines.md; every `x-audit-action` is in state-machines.md section 7 or flagged `x-audit-action-proposed`.
4. MFA (r7 1d): session states {none, password only, enrolment pending, MFA verified, MFA expired, after password reset, after authenticator reset pending, after break-glass} x every route with `MFA: yes`: only "MFA verified" reaches a handler.

## Mapping notes

- One operation per policy row. Where roles, scope and step-up are identical, several events share an operation and are selected by a discriminator: `transitionAbuseReport`, `transitionSuggestion`, `advanceMfaReset` (take, approve, deny), `respondToInvitation` (accept, decline), `respondOwnershipTransfer` (consent, decline), `decideClaim`, `decideAppeal`, `decideConflict`, `decideOwnershipTransfer`, `decideImportBatch`. Operations whose events need different roles are separate (suspend, revoke, reinstate; take, approve of break-glass).
- Second-factor reset: "request" and "submit recovery code" are the single machine event `mfa_reset.start` (`startMfaReset`, after the email link of the password-reset flow); "execute" is a system event fired by `advanceMfaReset` approve. See Issue 8.
- `viewEvidence`, `getCommitteeContacts`, `getRecoveryCaseContacts`, `searchGlobal`, `listAuditEvents` and `downloadOwnExport` are audited reads (`x-audit-read`).
